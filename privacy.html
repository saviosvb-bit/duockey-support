Duockey Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Duockey doesn’t collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No data collection. The app doesn’t collect personal data, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers.
  • No network access. The app doesn’t connect to the internet.
  • No tracking, ads or analytics. The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party code.
  • Playing a friend nearby stays between the two phones. When you use Play a Friend Nearby, Duockey uses Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the ultra wideband chip to find the other iPhone and measure how close it is. Moves and scores go straight between the two phones, encrypted. Each phone uses a random temporary name that changes every time, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Duockey asks permission for Local Network and Nearby Interaction first.
  • Settings stay on your device. Your choices for table, skill level, sound, vibration and drag mode are saved on your iPhone only, so they’re there next time. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Children. Duockey is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
