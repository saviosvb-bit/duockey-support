Duockey Support

How to play

Two players (iPhone Duo). Unfold the phone fully and lay it flat between you. The inner display is the table; the fold is the centre line. Drag your paddle on your half, and both tap Ready. First to 7 wins.

One player. On iPhone Duo’s front (outer) display, tap Play Solo on the “Unfold to Play” card and the match starts after a short intro. On any other iPhone, Duockey opens on its game card: tap Play. You’re the blue paddle at the bottom; red plays the top.

Second puck. Under your score is a puck button. Press it once a match and a second puck fades into the centre of the table. It vanishes when it goes into either goal, and that goal counts. While two pucks are in play, a goal doesn’t pause the game: play carries on until the last puck goes in.

Pause. Tap the pause button in your corner. Folding the phone pauses the game too, and it picks up where you left off.

Settings

Tap the gear on the front screen, on the Duockey card before a match, or on the pause card during a game:

  • Table: Rink or Pub Table.
  • Appearance: follows your iPhone’s Light or Dark setting (the Pub Table is always dark).
  • First to: how many points win a match, from 3 to 10 (7 to start). Changing it during a match starts a new game.
  • Skill: Easy, Medium or Hard. Changing it during a match starts a new game.
  • Sound and Vibration: on or off.
  • Drag Anywhere: your paddle moves by how far your finger moves, from wherever you touch, instead of jumping under your finger.

Questions

The game won’t start on the inner display. Open iPhone Duo all the way and lay it flat. Partly folded, the game waits and shows “Lay It Flat”.

There’s no sound. Check the Sound setting, and the Ring/Silent switch: Duockey follows it.

Accessibility. With Reduce Motion on, motion effects such as the puck’s trail are turned off; with Increase Contrast on, the table’s markings are stronger.
