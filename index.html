Duockey Support

How to play

Starting a game. Choose the computer’s difficulty (Easy, Medium or Hard) and the points to win (3, 5, 7 or 10) on the New Game card, then tap Start. A 3-2-1 counts you in; you can move your paddle during the count, but the puck waits until it ends.

One player. On iPhone Duo’s outer display, choose Solo on the “Choose How to Play” card. On any other iPhone, Duockey opens on the New Game card. You’re the blue paddle at the bottom; red is the computer.

Two players (iPhone Duo). Unfold the phone fully and lay it flat between you, then choose Duo. The inner display is the table and the fold is the centre line. Each of you drags your paddle on your own half; both tap Ready to start.

Power-ups. Under your score are three buttons, each usable once a match: a second puck that fades into the centre, slow time for everyone but you, and shake the table so the puck changes direction.

Pause. Tap the pause button in your corner. The pause card offers Resume, Restart (the same settings, straight into a new game) and Leave. Folding the phone pauses the game too, and it picks up where you left off.

Settings

Tap the gear on the start card, or on the pause card during a game. A preview shows your table and mallet:

  • Table: Rink, Pub Table or Galaxy, with the arrows. The Rink follows your iPhone’s Light or Dark setting; the Pub Table and Galaxy are always dark.
  • Mallet: Classic, Arcade, Neon or Glass, with the arrows.
  • Sound and Haptics: tap to turn on or off.

Difficulty and points to win are chosen on the New Game card.

Questions

The game won’t start on the inner display. Open iPhone Duo all the way and lay it flat. Partly folded, the game waits and shows “Lay It Flat”.

There’s no sound. Check the Sound setting, and the Ring/Silent switch: Duockey follows it.

Accessibility. With Reduce Motion on, motion effects such as the puck’s trail are turned off; with Increase Contrast on, the table’s markings are stronger. Duockey is played by dragging a paddle, so it needs touch.
